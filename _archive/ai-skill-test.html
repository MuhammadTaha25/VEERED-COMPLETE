Assessment Purpose. This 35-question AI Skill Assessment is drawn at random from a 290-question bank and evaluates demonstrated competence in AI-related skills. It is Stage 1 of a two-stage evaluation: pass at 70% and you'll be emailed an invitation to Stage 2, with a 24-hour window to complete it.

Human Verification. Per EU GDPR Article 22 and EU AI Act Articles 14 & 86, every assessment result is verified by a trained Human AI Consultant before any recommendation is issued. No outcome is ever issued by the system alone.

Data Protection. Your data (first name, last name, DOB) was collected at Step 2 (CV upload) and is used solely for the 7-day lock mechanism and result linkage. No biometric data, video analysis, emotion recognition, or voice-print processing is performed.

Your Rights. You may request a reassessment from a Human AI Consultant through the platform dashboard. A new assessment may be scheduled after a minimum 7-day interval.

Lock Mechanism. To maintain fairness, candidates scoring below 70% are locked for 7 days. The lock is matched by name and DOB provided at Step 2.

Consent. By continuing, you confirm you have read these terms and consent to processing in accordance with EU GDPR and the EU AI Act high-risk system requirements.
