4. Assessment integrity

To keep results fair and comparable across candidates, timed assessments are server-timed and cannot be paused or extended by closing your browser. Stage 2 (coding) must be completed without generative AI tools, external assistance, or another person — focus changes and similar signals are recorded as contextual evidence for human review, and are never treated as automatic proof of misconduct. Confirmed unauthorised assistance may end your candidacy for that role.
