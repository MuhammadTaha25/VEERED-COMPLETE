🔒 How we keep you secure

Industry-standard controls applied to every company account.

  • TLS
    Encrypted in transit. HTTPS/TLS 1.2+ on every request; HSTS enforced.
  • HASH
    Passwords never stored. Hashed with Argon2id + per-user salt server-side.
  • CSRF
    CSRF-protected forms. Synchroniser-token on all state-changing requests.
  • 2FA
    Optional MFA. TOTP / authenticator app enforced for admin seats.
  • RATE
    Brute-force defence. Rate-limited login with progressive lockout & captcha.
  • GDPR
    UK & EU data residency. Data stored in UK/Ireland; right to export & erase.
  • COOKIE
    Hardened sessions. HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite cookies with short idle timeout.
  • PCI
    Payments handled by Stripe. PCI-DSS Level 1; we never see or store card numbers.

Data residency

Our approved architecture stores all company and candidate data in an EU West (Ireland) region, with no transfer of personal data outside the UK/EEA without appropriate safeguards (Art. 44–49). This prototype build is not yet connected to that production database — see our full Privacy Policy for current status.

Your rights

Right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15–22). Contact us to exercise any of these — full detail in our Privacy Policy.

Lawful basis

We process under legitimate interest for recruitment matching and consent where required. Candidates are never charged to apply — that is the law here and our policy everywhere.
